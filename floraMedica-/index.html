Safety-first identification

A plant match is a clue, not permission to consume.

FloraMedica is built to make dangerous botanical ambiguity visible. Always confirm identification with qualified local expertise and authoritative references before ingesting or using a wild plant medicinally.

▲ Lookalike interlock

Similar appearance ≠ same plant

Field identification can be affected by lighting, season, plant age, missing organs, image quality, and regional variation.

Use multiple plant organs whenever possible and treat toxic-lookalike alerts as a stop signal.
